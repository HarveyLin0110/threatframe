# STRIDE Guide

Use STRIDE as a thinking aid, not a quota.

- Spoofing — impersonation.
- Tampering — unauthorized modification.
- Repudiation — denial of accountable actions.
- Information Disclosure — unauthorized exposure.
- Denial of Service — degradation/unavailability.
- Elevation of Privilege — unauthorized privilege gain.

Preferred scenario:

"When [actor/source] uses [interface/condition] to [action], [asset/property] may be compromised, causing [consequence]."

Do not generate six threats per element just to fill categories.

Traceability:
`Asset / Interface / Boundary → T → AP if needed → M → SR → V → Residual Risk`

CAPEC/ATT&CK/CWE mappings enrich this chain; they do not replace it.
